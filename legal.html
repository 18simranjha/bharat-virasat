🛡️ Privacy Policy

BharatVirasat is a digital heritage preservation platform and exploratory archive. We respect your autonomy and privacy and do not sell personal information to third parties.

  • Account Authentication: When you register or sign in using Firebase Authentication (Email/Password or Google Sign-In), your profile is secured with encrypted industry credentials.
  • Private Scrapbook & Journal: Field photographs, notes, and visit dates recorded in your Sacred Scrapbook are private to your authenticated user account.
  • AI Heritage Guide & Voice Interaction: Questions submitted to the Gemini AI Guide or Dadi Storyteller are processed securely via our backend to generate cultural narratives. Do not submit sensitive passwords or financial credentials.
  • RakshaSetu Location Tools: If you grant permission, your device location is used in your browser to show your position on the map and prepare Google Maps, SMS, and WhatsApp links. Loading map tiles may reveal the approximate displayed area to the map tile provider. The site does not verify nearby facilities, send your coordinates to its backend, or automatically notify emergency services or contacts. Your location is included in an external link only if you choose that link; the destination app or service then handles it.
  • Browser Storage: Preferences such as dark mode, favorite monuments, and cached itineraries may be stored in your browser's localStorage for instant offline access.
  • Third-Party Integrations: Google Translate loads on demand for multi-lingual access. Interactive maps rely on OpenStreetMap and Leaflet.

For questions, data deletion requests, or privacy clarifications, email our team at rsgaming8090@gmail.com.

📜 Terms and Conditions

  • Informational Purpose: Monument schedules, ASI entry fees, SAARC slabs, and travel routes are informational. Always cross-check current regulations with official ASI offices or local administrative authorities before travel.
  • Cultural Respect: All cultural stories, folk narratives, and architectural models celebrate the living traditions of India. Visitors are urged to practice ethical tourism, maintain quiet in sanctum sanctorum, and never deface heritage monuments.
  • AI Output Notice: While backed by cutting-edge Gemini models, AI generated itineraries and responses are intended as cultural companions and should not be treated as absolute legal, medical, or emergency directives.
  • Service Availability: The platform is provided as an open cultural innovation project on an "as available" basis without warranties of uninterrupted uptime.

🍪 Cookie & Storage Policy

BharatVirasat prioritizes privacy-first architecture. We utilize browser localStorage for critical client preferences:

  • Theme Preferences: Remembers your light or dark mode choice.
  • Favorites: Preserves bookmarked monuments in your local archive.
  • Localization: Google Translate operates client-side to dynamically render text into 28+ regional and international languages.

You can reset all local storage preferences at any time by clearing your browser data or using site data settings.

🚨 Emergency & Safety Disclaimer

RakshaSetu provides location and contact shortcuts only. It is not an emergency dispatch service and does not monitor or verify local safety conditions:

  • In life-threatening situations, dial the official National Emergency Response Support System immediately: 112 (National ERSS) or 1363 (Tourist Helpline).
  • This site does not send alerts to emergency services or contacts. Calls, SMS, and WhatsApp links require your action and depend on your device, network, and configured apps.
  • Device GPS availability and accuracy vary. Always confirm the location shown before sharing it.
