Privacy policy

BharatVirasat is a heritage exploration website. We do not sell personal information. Advertising and analytics services may be added as the site develops, and this policy explains the related third-party technology.

  • Information you provide: a name, rating, and review text when you submit a visitor review. Reviews are sent to the review API and may be publicly displayed. Do not include contact details, sensitive information, or information about another person.
  • AI chat: your question, selected age group, and recent chat messages are sent to the configured Gemini API through our backend to generate a response. Do not submit secrets or sensitive personal information.
  • Browser storage: preferences, dark-mode choice, locally saved fallback reviews, and consent choices may be stored in your browser using localStorage. This is not a server account.
  • Technical data: the hosting provider may process ordinary request data such as IP address, timestamps, and error logs for security and delivery. The site does not use this data for behavioral profiling.
  • Third parties: Google Translate loads only after optional language-service consent. Google Gemini receives chat requests when you use AI Chat. Google Maps opens only when you choose directions. Their own policies apply.
  • Advertising and analytics: if Google AdSense or analytics services are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to measure traffic, show ads, limit repeated ads, and personalize advertising where permitted. These services may process information such as approximate location, browser details, and interaction with ads.
  • Retention: public reviews may remain until removed by the site operator. Chat history is kept in the current browser session by the interface and is sent with later chat messages; the app does not intentionally save it in a server account.

For deletion, privacy, corrections, or collaboration questions, contact the site operator at rsgaming8090@gmail.com.

For information about Google's partner technology, see Google's partner-site policy. You can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

Google Translate is an optional third-party service and may set its own cookies after you allow the language service. If AdSense or analytics is enabled, the relevant provider may set cookies or use similar technologies under its own privacy policy and cookie controls.

Visitors in regions that require consent may be shown a Google-certified consent message before personalized advertising is served. Advertising should remain disabled until the required consent configuration and publisher disclosures are in place.
